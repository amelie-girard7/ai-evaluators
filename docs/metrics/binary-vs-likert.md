# Binary Evals vs Likert Scales: The Evidence

Why binary scoring outperforms 5-star ratings for AI evaluation reliability

## 7.1 The Problem with Likert Scales
Likert scales (1-5 or 1-10 ratings) are intuitive but produce unreliable evals. Decodingai.com's research ('The 5-Star Lie') demonstrates why: different evaluators disagree on the difference between a 3 and a 4; LLM judges tend to cluster around the midpoint (3/5); the signal-to-noise ratio is low; and scores cannot be aggregated meaningfully across different aspects of quality.

## 7.2 Designing Binary Criteria for Client Feedback Classification
Each binary criterion should be answerable YES or NO by any domain expert who reads the feedback and the output. If two experts would disagree on whether the answer is YES or NO, the criterion is not binary enough - it needs to be more specific.
